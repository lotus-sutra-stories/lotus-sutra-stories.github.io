39:T463,Zhu Daosheng, ordained under Zhu Fatai, dwelt seven years in seclusion on Mount Lu, constantly reciting the Lotus Sutra. He later studied under Kumārajīva in Guanzhong; his dialectic surpassed all, and men called his understanding divine. Lecturing at Huqiu on the theme that even icchantikas possess buddha-nature, the very stones nodded. At Bantang, while he recited the sutra, a pheasant came regularly to listen; he later heard it had been reborn as a boy who ordained, recited the Lotus, and died young — at the grave a light shone one night, and on opening it a tongue was found bearing a green lotus, whereupon a stupa and later a monastery arose (today's Bantang Monastery). His theses — 'merit receives no recompense' and 'awakening is sudden' — the textualists damned as heresy; so he swore before the assembly: if his teaching did not contradict the sutras, he would die seated upon the lion seat.
