18:T80c,# Tanwujie: Reciting the Universal Gate Chapter, Lion and Vulture Scatter the Beasts

> Recorded in the "Eminent Monks" section of the *Xianying lu* under "Tripitaka Master Jie", citing the *Fayuan zhulin* (Forest of Gems in the Garden of the Dharma). The *Chiyan ji* does not carry it. The English rendering follows the *Xianying lu*.

## The Story

In the early years of the Yuanjia era of the Liu Song, there was a monk of Huanglong named Tanwujie, austere and diligent in practice, who constantly recited the Universal Gate chapter of the Lotus Sutra. With twenty-five followers he journeyed west to India in search of the Dharma, passing through every kind of danger, his resolve growing firmer with each hardship.

On the road near Srāvastī the party met a herd of great elephants blocking the way. Tanwujie recited the Universal Gate chapter with devout heart, whereupon a lion charged out of the forest and the elephants bolted in every direction.

On another occasion a herd of wild buffaloes came roaring down, on the very point of trampling the travellers. Tanwujie recited with the same vigour as before; suddenly a great vulture swooped from the sky, the buffaloes scattered without a trace, and the party escaped harm.

## Editor's Reflection

The structure of this account corresponds exactly to the promise of the Universal Gate chapter: "If surrounded by evil beasts with sharp teeth and claws... by the power of remembering Avalokiteśvara they flee swiftly to the ends of the earth." The lion routing the elephants and the vulture scattering the buffaloes amounts to a staging of the verse. For the compiler it is a textbook case of scripture and response confirming one another: first the sutra's promise, then the traveller's record.

The reader should also note: this is an incidental record within a pilgrimage narrative. The protagonist's proper business is fetching scriptures; reciting the Universal Gate chapter is protective practice for the road. The response is not the goal — walking the road of Dharma-seeking in safety is.
