Master Kejiu: Names on the Lotus Platforms, Verified Five Years On

Person：Kejiu

Period：Northern Song (d. after 1093)

Died and revived in the eighth Yuanyou year (1093); a close friend of Su Shi.

Reading preferences

事見《法華經持驗記》卷二宋朝「宋釋可久」條。《顯應錄》成書於宋，未及收此（原書止於南宋前人物）。以下白話譯註依《持驗記》。

故事

可久法師，住四明開元寺，常誦《法華經》，時人稱他「久法華師」。他與蘇文忠公（蘇軾）交情最深，常住西湖祥符寺，一室蕭然，清介守貧。

一年元宵，蘇軾在九曲觀觀燈，隨從走散，獨行至可久室中——室內了無燈燭，只聞瞻蔔花香猶存。蘇軾仰天歎息，留詩一首，其中有「不把琉璃閒照佛，始知無盡本非燈」之句。

元祐八年（1093），可久無疾坐化。三日之後，忽然復甦，自述：「我已神遊淨土，親見彼土之相，與《十六觀經》所說的莊嚴完全相同。七寶池中所有蓮臺，都寫著當往生者的姓名。有一座紫金臺，寫的是成都府廣教院勳公——他已經往生其中了。又有一座金臺，寫的是明州孫十二郎；一座寫的是可久。又有一座銀臺，寫的是明州徐道姑。」

說完，他再度化去。

其後五年，徐道姑命終，異香滿室；又過七年，孫十二郎去世，時人聞天樂迎空——都與可久所說一一相合。

編者省思

這則記載的結構在感應錄中相當罕見：主角自己復甦作證，而「預言」在五年、七年後由他人的死亡瑞相逐一印證。原書收錄他，等於同時收錄了三則往生記載。他一生唯以誦《法華經》見稱，並無淨土行法的細節記載；然其神遊所記，全依《觀無量壽經》的淨土圖像。編者將其歸入「往生極樂」主題，標為「經解讀推斷」，並註記其信願欄位為「不明」（原文未記其發願語）。

附帶一提：蘇軾與誦經僧的這段往來（「無盡燈」之歎），是宋代士大夫與法華信仰交涉的生動側影。

Master Bacheng: Sixty Years of Recitation, a Single Prayer for the West

Bacheng (printed in the source with a variant glyph of 跋)|Eastern Jin (Chang'an)

Bacheng became a monk at twenty with dull faculties, vowing to recite the Lotus Sutra for rebirth in the West. He memorised a line a day, sometimes half a verse, completing the sutra only at eighty. When a heavenly envoy came to invite him to Trāyastriṃśā he declined — his prayer was solely for Ultimate Bliss. He later dreamt of a jeweled pagoda and the welcoming stairway of the Western jeweled city, arranged a communal offering, asked his disciples whether they saw the thousand Buddhas and smelled the uncanny fragrance, then passed away facing west.

Master Huiming: A Vow Set Westward, "All Saw the Buddha Come"

Huiming|Northern Zhou (d. 569)

Master Huiming of Northern Zhou memorised the Lotus Sutra at fifteen and devoted himself to the repentance rites. Studying meditation with Chan master Fayin, the two walked one evening in a pine grove, talking and smiling as they set their vow westward. In the eleventh month of the third Tianhe year he sat cross-legged facing west reciting the Buddha's name; everyone present saw the Buddha come as he joined his palms and passed away amid uncanny fragrance and music. Eight days later Fayin too died seated, with the same auspicious signs.

Liu Yimin: "By the Power of This Sutra", Passing Away Facing West

Liu Chengzhi, styled Yimin|Eastern Jin (d. 410)

Liu Chengzhi of the Eastern Jin left office for Mount Lu, joining Huiyuan's Lotus Society to practise the Pure Land path together. In the samādhi of Buddha-recollection he repeatedly saw the Buddha's light, Amitābha's welcoming touch, and himself drinking the water of eight virtues in the jeweled pool. At the end he offered incense before the sacred images — first to Śākyamuni, then to Amitābha, and third to the Lotus Sutra: "By the power of this sutra I shall be reborn." He passed away facing west with palms joined; the fragrance lasted seven days.

Master Daoang: Declining Tuṣita, Choosing the West

Daoang|Sui–early Tang (Xiangzhou)

Master Daoang of Kaiyuan Monastery in Xiangzhou regularly lectured on the Lotus Sutra. Mid-lecture one day, a host of sages with banners and music descended from the sky, announcing that the Tuṣita heaven had come especially to welcome him. 'Heaven is the root of birth-and-death,' he replied; 'I have never aspired to it — my aspiration is the West alone.' They vanished. Presently music resounded from the west; he charged the assembly to 'recite Amitābha diligently and be born together in the pure land', the incense burner dropped from his hand, and he closed his eyes on the high seat. The source cites the Wangsheng lüezhuan.
