Master Daosheng: Sudden Awakening, Dying Seated upon the Lion Seat

Person：Zhu Daosheng

Period：Eastern Jin (d. 434)

Of Julu; ordained under Zhu Fatai and took his master's surname, Zhu.

Reading preferences

事見《法華經顯應錄》「高僧」門「虎丘生法師」條，並見《法華經持驗記》宋朝「宋釋道生」條。以下白話譯註以《顯應錄》為主，《持驗記》異文及高啟詩見考證。

故事

道生法師，從師姓竺。年少志學之年，談吐清新，人皆畏服。他認為入道之要，以慧解為本，所以鑽研群經，不憚勞苦。後入關中，稟承羅什之學，辯問超卓，時人咸稱「神悟」。

（《持驗記》云：生公初入廬山，幽棲七年，常誦《法華經》；後在虎丘講經，講至「闡提皆有佛性」處，頑石皆為點頭。）

一時，他住在半塘，誦《法華經》。忽然有一隻雉，常常飛來聽受。有一天雉不見了，道生還掛念著。夜裡，雉入夢說：「某因聽經，已獲改轉報身，今生在某家作兒子。待過幾年，再來奉事法師。」後來道生詢問，果然如此。孩子出家之後不久，童子之年便命終了，葬於林中。

一天夜裡，葬處忽然放光，輝照塘塢。鄉人驚異，開墓查看——得到一條舌頭，舌上生著青色蓮華。於是起塔，後來擴建成寺，就是今天的半塘寺。

道生曾立論：「善不受報，頓悟成佛。」守文墨守的舊學，把這當作邪說。道生對眾發誓說：「若我所立之義不違經旨，捨壽之時，當踞師子座。」

後來《大般涅槃經》譯出，果然與他所說相符。道生於是升座開講，聽者悟悅。法席將畢，麈尾忽然紛然墜地——道生端坐正容而逝，如同入定一般。

時人語曰：「生叡發天真」——稱讚秀出者，說的就是這樣的人。

編者省思

道生是中國佛教史上「以理抗經、終為經證」的經典案例：闡提有性、頓悟成佛，在當時皆屬離經叛道；一代新經譯出，竟全如所料。《持驗記》所記「頑石點頭」，與「踞師子座」同為中國佛教最有名的兩個「驗證」意象，本站收錄的正是後者的原典出處系統之一。

半塘舌上青蓮一節，與法志座下的聽經雉（S015）同型而異事：同是禽鳥聽《法華》得轉人身，此處更進一層——童子夭後，舌生青蓮。編者將兩則並列收錄，正見此一母題在感應錄中的反覆變奏。麈尾墜地、端坐而逝的收束，不涉往生語，本站如實歸入「現世安樂／悟入」而往生欄位作「未明言」。

The Bodhisattva Never Disparaging: "I Dare Not Disparage You"

The monk Never Disparaging (a previous incarnation of Śākyamuni Buddha)|Remote past, within the Lotus Sutra narrative (during the semblance-dharma of Buddha Bhīṣmagarjitasvara)

As told in the Lotus Sutra: in the semblance-dharma age a monk bowed to everyone he met, saying "I dare not disparage you, for you shall all become Buddhas." Though mocked and stoned, he never altered his practice; at the end of his life he heard the Lotus Sutra proclaimed in open space and attained purity of the six senses. The Buddha identifies that monk as his own former self.

Master Fatai: The Sutra That Fell into the River and Did Not Get Wet

Fatai|Sui dynasty (Meizhou)

Fatai of Meizhou in Sui times constantly recited the Lotus Sutra and copied it out by hand. Sent to Yizhou for mounting, the scroll fell from the Zao bridge into the water and could not be found; he wept and searched along the bank — then saw a bundle on a small islet. It was the sutra, held up by grass and branches, without a single wet spot. Mounted with sandalwood rollers and returned, it filled the room each night with uncanny fragrance. Master Biao, visiting his recitation hall at night, saw a crowd kneeling with palms joined — and withdrew in a sweat.

The Monk Fada: 'When the Mind Awakens, It Turns the Lotus Sutra'

Fada; the Sixth Patriarch Huineng|Tang dynasty (Caoxi)

Fada of Hongzhou left home at seven and spent his life reciting the Lotus Sutra. Visiting the Sixth Patriarch at Caoxi, he was asked what the sutra's purport was; told to unfold and read it, he had barely begun the Parable chapter when the Patriarch stopped him, expounded the 'one great matter' as the Buddha's insight — 'your own mind itself, nothing else besides' — and gave the verse 'when the mind is deluded it is turned by the Lotus Sutra; when the mind awakens, it turns the Lotus Sutra'. Fada wept, awakened, and confessed: 'Three thousand recitations of the sutra — undone by one sentence at Caoxi.'
