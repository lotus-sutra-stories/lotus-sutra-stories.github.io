The two primary works behind this collection, with bibliographic details.

Fahua jing xianying lu (Records of Evident Responses to the Lotus Sutra)

法華經顯應錄

CBETA X78, No. 1540

Compiled by Zongxiao (Song dynasty)|Southern Song|Vol. 2

Bibliographic structure

Two fascicles arranged by category of person: prefaced by a foreword and table of contents; juan 1 opens with "Ancient Sages" (seven narratives from the sutra itself) and "Eminent Monks"; juan 2 continues the monks, then "Eminent Nuns", "Laymen", "Laywomen", closing with a colophon. The internal table of contents is titled Fahua jing yan-ying lu (with two characters transposed); this bibliographic variant is recorded as printed.

Fahua jing chiyan ji (Records of Verified Experiences of Upholding the Lotus Sutra)

法華經持驗記

CBETA X78, No. 1541

Compiled by Zhou Kefu (Qing dynasty)|Qing|Vol. 2

Bibliographic structure

Two fascicles arranged chronologically by dynasty: prefaced by the compiler's foreword and an exhortation to circulate the collection; the main text runs from the Six Dynasties (Jin) through Song, Qi, Liang, Chen, Northern Wei, Northern Qi, Northern Zhou, Sui and Tang (juan 1), to Wuyue, the Five Dynasties, Song, Yuan and Ming (juan 2). Its arrangement differs from the categorical order of the Xianying lu; where both works carry the same narrative, this site preserves both primary witnesses separately.

Project disclosure

Lotus Sutra Stories is an independent, non-commercial, freely accessible Buddhist literature project presenting historical accounts of upholding and reciting the Lotus Sutra, particularly from the Fahua jing xianying lu and Fahua jing chiyan ji. We distinguish primary texts, modern Traditional Chinese explanations, English translations, and editorial reflections. Miraculous events are presented as accounts recorded in religious literature, not as independently established scientific or historical facts or guarantees of particular worldly or rebirth outcomes. This project is not officially affiliated with or endorsed by the religious organizations, archives, databases or institutions referenced. Third-party text transcriptions, translations and artwork remain subject to their applicable licences and attribution requirements.

This site has not yet been formally deployed. GitHub Pages hosting and a custom domain remain plans, and are not live unless confirmed by the project owner.
